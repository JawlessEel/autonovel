TITLE: Saltwater Ledger
HOOK: On the Corpus Christi bayfront, a harbor clerk named Mara Quin can smell when a shipping ledger is lying — and tonight every page smells like diesel and copper.
WORLD: Present-day Corpus Christi, TX. Working docks, tourist piers, refinery haze, pelicans on pylons. The speculative twist is quiet: some people (rare) can taste falsehoods in paper and ink — a "ledger sense" that costs them a nosebleed and a day of flat taste afterward.
MAGIC/COST: Ledger sense works only on handwritten or printed commercial records, not speech. Overuse = nosebleed, temporary anosmia, and one true memory from childhood goes blurry for a week.
TENSION: PERSONAL — Mara must decide whether to flag a falsified bunker-fuel invoice that implicates her uncle's tug. COSMIC — a string of "clean" manifests are covering a slow poison dump into the ship channel.
THEME: What do you owe family when the paper that feeds them is the thing killing the water?
WHY IT'S NOT GENERIC: No dragons; Gulf Coast logistics noir with a sensory lie-detector tied to paperwork.
TONE: Mara Quin slice — wry, sensory, grounded, PG-13. No graphic violence or explicit content for this smoke seed.
